# CLUECREW LIVE LAUNCH PACK — v3.0
### Supersedes DEMAND-TEST-PACK v1/v2. This is a LIVE product page, launched in two steps.
**Ratified pivot: no waitlist theatre. Crew (free) opens as a real product; Full Crew checkout opens when the paid-content floor and legal review are done. The page is honest about exactly what's live today — honesty-as-roadmap is the launch's brand moment.**

---

## 1. THE TWO-STEP LAUNCH

**Step 1 — "Crew opens" (gates: §5A).** The free tier goes live for real: signup (parent + child profile, no card, Children's Code flows as built), the free ten VR cases with all five Modes, Word Vault drip, weekly Boss Round. The page's primary CTA is **"Start free — no card, no clock"**. This is the Duolingo move completed: the button delivers the product.

**Step 2 — "Full Crew opens" (gates: §5B, target stated on-page as a month, not a date).** Paid checkout enables at the ratified prices. Until then, the pricing table is fully visible with a **"Reserve the founding rate"** capture on Full Crew — name + email, explicitly *not* a payment, rate honoured at opening. *(This preserves the original demand signal, upgraded: paid-intent expressed by people who can already touch the free product is far stronger data than a cold waitlist.)*

**Hidden at launch:** Crew Plus (needs a bench; appears when it exists) and Summer Intensive (it's for summer 2027; reappears spring 2027). Neither is mentioned — absent, not "coming soon", to keep the page clean.
