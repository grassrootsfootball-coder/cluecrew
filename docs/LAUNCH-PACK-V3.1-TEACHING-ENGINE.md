## NEW SECTION B — "Under the bonnet" (for the parent who wants to know)

Header: **Built like it matters. Because it does.**
Sub: *For the parents who want to know exactly what they're signing their child up to — here's the engine, plainly.*
Six short entries, plain prose, each one specific and verifiable:
1. **Review timed to the forgetting curve.** Every session opens by revisiting what your child learned before — spaced at the intervals memory research says they'd otherwise start to forget, tightening automatically as the exam approaches.
2. **Difficulty that keeps them in the zone.** The engine holds your child where they succeed roughly three times out of four — hard enough to grow, never hard enough to break. Two misses in a row and it eases off and offers another way in. Three, and it changes the subject. No child grinds against a wall here.
3. **Every wrong answer is authored.** Each distractor is built from a real, named misconception — so a miss produces the hint for that exact mix-up, not a generic "try again". A teacher signs off every question before any child sees it.
4. **Mock exams that wait until they're ready.** Papers unlock only when your child has been taught every question type on them and their exam-format accuracy says they're ready. A mock should measure readiness — never ambush.
5. **Intensity that follows the calendar.** A Year 4 gets foundations at a gentle pace; the final four months before the exam introduce nothing new — consolidation only. The plan changes; the 15-minute cap never does.
6. **The exam format, made boring.** Every session ends with real exam-format questions, no tools, no theming — so by September, test formatting is the most familiar thing in the room.
Footer line + link: *"Want the full picture? We publish how ClueCrew teaches — every rule above, and the evidence behind it. The answer is never 'trade secret'." → /how-we-teach*
