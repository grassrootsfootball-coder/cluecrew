## TO SPLIT (3 → 6)

### M-04 → three families
Wrong-operation covers three different children and they need different hints:
1. **Chose the wrong operation** — sharing where it should be grouping, or the reverse.
2. **Reversed a division** — right operation, wrong order. The child divides the wrong way round. Her hint: "check which number goes inside the bus stop."
3. **Misread which quantity is which** — took the wrong number as the divisor or the multiplier.
Her reasoning: a child who divides in the wrong direction has the right operation and the wrong order. She needs different words from a child who chose sharing instead of grouping. The hints differ, so the diagnoses differ, so the families differ.

### M-05 → separate the scale-reading
Reading a value off an axis is **not** a multiplicative comparison. It sits in "ratio and proportion" only because the corpus filed it there. Different skill, different failure mode, own family.

### M-06 → separate bare-number from worded
A bare fraction-of-amount is one step. A worded one is two, and the two-step version is where stopping halfway becomes possible. Bundled, a T2 signature would cover items that are genuinely T3 work.
