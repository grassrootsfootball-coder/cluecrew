# The deferred queue — improvements ruled out of an act, not out of existence

**Opened 2026-08-19.** Every entry here is something a reviewer or I noticed while doing a different
job, judged **not part of that job**, and set down rather than folded in.

**Why this file exists.** The failure it closes is not forgetting — it is the opposite. An
improvement noticed mid-repair is at its most persuasive exactly when it is least examined: it is
adjacent, it is small, and the person holding it is already editing the row. Folding it in makes the
act's own record wrong, because the ruling that authorised the repair did not authorise the
improvement, and six months later nothing distinguishes them. **R62's coupling is the mechanism:
re-deriving a LIVE reviewed item on the strength of an adjacent good idea is a change nobody
decided.**

A deferral is not a rejection. Each entry names **who it belongs to** and **what would move it**.

---

## D1 — `tired` as a wrong-strength distractor on `bank-vr-04-closest-meaning-30`

**Owner: Annie. Opened 2026-08-19 alongside R336.**

`tired` was the key for **exhausted** and has been replaced by `worn out`. It is now a free string,
and it is the textbook `vr04-wrong-strength` distractor for that stem — right sense, wrong degree,
which is precisely the confusion the tag exists to catch.

**Why it was not done in the same act.** R336 repaired a wrong key. Adding a distractor changes the
option set of a **LIVE reviewed item**, on my judgement rather than hers, in a bank whose direction
is already unverified. **The repair was owed four times over; the improvement was owed to nobody.**

**What moves it:** Annie ruling on the option set, ideally in the same pass that resolves the
provisional key when a frequency signal lands.

## D2 — `well-behaved` is the only hyphenated option among three single words

**Owner: Annie. Opened 2026-08-19 alongside R336.**

On `bank-vr-04-closest-meaning-23` (obedient), the authored key `well-behaved` is the sole
two-part option. **A shape a child can see without reading is a shortcut**, and this is the family
of fault the generated-distractor rule in CLAUDE.md exists to hold down: a feature that is both
predictable and unique to the key.

**It may be nothing.** Hyphenation is far weaker than position or frequency as a tell, and no
measurement has been taken — I am recording an unexamined suspicion, and saying so.

**What moves it:** either a measurement across the bank (how many keys are hyphenated versus how
many distractors), or Annie ruling the shape acceptable. **Not a redraft on my reading of it** — the
key is hers and it is three days old.

## D3 — The declaration SETS are not compared in either direction

**Owner: Cowork and this store jointly. Opened 2026-08-20 alongside R339.**

Both gates key on a declared quoted span, and neither store can see the other's declarations. Four
texts here have their pass/fail decided by the declaration alone, swinging 11 to 14 words, one of them
LIVE. **Two correct gates reading different rows will refuse each other's items forever**, and
converging the counting rule does not touch it.

**Opened as its own thread on 2026-08-20 and now runnable**: `pnpm check:declaration-sets` (R341).
It exports our 157 declarations on every run so the return leg travels regardless, prints
**NOT RECEIVED** rather than a zero until Cowork's set lands, and compares in **both directions**
plus the third class a one-way diff misses — same item and field, different span text, where both
stores believe they have declared the same thing.

**Each divergence is classified ACTIVE or LATENT.** The four R339 found are only the ones sitting at
a cap boundary; a divergence on a comfortably short sentence agrees today **by length, not by rule**,
and becomes active the moment someone edits that sentence — with nothing to connect the edit to the
refusal that follows.

**What moves it:** Cowork's declaration set arriving as item id, field and span text — requested
alongside the cap rule. The check runs itself once it does.

## How an entry leaves

Ruled on, or measured and dismissed. **An entry that is simply old does not leave**, and this file
is not tidied — a deferral that quietly expires is the fold-in it was written to prevent, one step
slower.
