# THE CLUECREW CHARACTER & STORY BIBLE
### v1.2 — consolidated. Supersedes v1.1. Story format ratified: PROSE CHAPTERS ("a book the child is in"), not comic pages.
**Sits under the manifesto. Three governing laws, outranking everything below:**
**LAW 1 — DIEGESIS AT CASE LEVEL.** Story wraps cases, sequencing and ceremonies. Item stems are never rewritten for narrative. Plain mode carries zero story (P4).
**LAW 2 — TENSION LAW.** Suspense lives in the mystery, never in the child's performance. No answer, score or result ever raises, lowers or colours story stakes. Warm resolutions are mandatory.
**LAW 3 — STORY NEVER TAXES PRACTICE.** Daily case intros ≤30s. Rank/season CHAPTERS are opt-in, 2–4 minutes' reading at age ≤9, skippable, replayable from the case-file shelf, and never gate practice (D5, amended).
**LAW 4 — THE BOOK IS ALSO READ ALOUD.** Every chapter ships with full audio (pre-generated, cached). A text story mode without audio would exclude the dyslexic and EAL children this product champions; audio is a release condition per chapter, not an enhancement.
**LAW 5 — WORDS ARE COLLECTIBLE IN THE PROSE.** Chapters are seeded with Word Vault tier words; any seeded word is tappable (definition card, collect to Vault). Reading feeds collecting; collecting motivates reading.
**FORMAT (ratified): prose chapters in second person — "a book the child is in" — with one spot illustration per chapter head (placeholder art until the illustrator delivers). Comic pages are dead; the illustrator commission's deliverable 4 becomes a chapter-head spot-illustration set (6–8 pieces).**

## 1. THE WORLD
**Ashline** — a lamplit, rain-glossed city, cosy-noir register: long coats, warm windows, tea in the incident room. Timeless-contemporary. Flat modern vector, manifesto palette; rain rendered as warmth (glow, reflections), never gloom. The quarters are the Districts: **Cipher Quarter** (VR, teal) · **the Workshop** (Maths, green) · **the Gallery** (NVR, violet) · **the Archive** (English, rose) · **Crew HQ** at the centre, amber-lit.

## 2. THE CAST (working names — IPO/trademark sanity-check before production art)
- **The child** — the newest recruit, played through their avatar. Base look chosen at signup (skin, hair, starter coat); outfits TRANSFORM at each rank AND accessories are EARNED from signature cases. Light dialogue choices at story beats: 2–3 options, all in-voice, colouring scenes only — never gating content, never writing to learning state.
- **Chief Inspector Nia Marlowe** — runs HQ. Calm, dry, sees everything, says a quarter of it. Voices rank ceremonies and Board invitations. Never explains a concept; she assigns, trusts, promotes.
- **Ozzie Finch** — Cipher Quarter's legendary codebreaker; brilliant-but-scatty; THE TEACH-BACK TARGET. His confident wrong steps are the misconception library personified — he errs exactly as children do, and takes correction with delight: "Ha — sharper than I was at your rank." Carries the reactive 8-state rig beside the play area (the existing `mascotController` re-targets to him unchanged).
- **Marta Okafor-Bright** — the Workshop's quartermaster. Builds, measures, prices; bar models are her blueprints. Maths season anchor.
- **Silas Vane** — the Gallery's observer. Rotations, reflections, the shape of things; speaks rarely, points precisely. NVR anchor; deadpan.
- **Prue Archer** — the Archive's keeper. Words, records, old stories; English anchor and in-world custodian of the Word Vault.
- **The Grey Umbrella** — the mystery figure. Season one: cases keep resolving a beat after someone unseen got there first; only a small grey umbrella token remains. **[Sealed two-year reveal: David's private appendix — not in this repo.]** Every season plants exactly one clue. Tension-Law compliant: the payoff is an honour.

## 3. SEASONS AND THE THROUGH-THREAD
One season per district. Each season RESOLVES COMPLETELY (1-Year and Summer children get whole stories) while advancing one Grey Umbrella thread.
- **S1 — Cipher Quarter (VR, launch): "The Rainmaker Letters."** Coded messages across Ashline predict small impossible things. Every VR engine family is a lead: the codes themselves (CODEBREAKER) · words hidden in shop signs (STOWAWAY) · the letters' strange word-choices (WORD WEB) · the pattern between recipients (BRIDGE) · witness statements that don't add up (DEDUCTION DEN). Resolves warm — the letters are invitations, not threats. First umbrella token found.
- **S2 — the Workshop (Maths): "The Weight of the Matter."** The city's measures are off — recipes, bridges, market scales. Every strand is an audit lead. Marta's season.
- **S3 — the Gallery (NVR): "The Mirror Thief."** Artworks subtly transformed — rotated, reflected, resequenced. Silas's season.
- **S4 — the Archive (English): "The Unfinished Story."** A serial story appears with pages missing; comprehension and writing recover it. Prue's season; feeds the Writing Room.

## 4. DIEGETIC LEARNING — HOW IT WORKS
- **Case shell carries the story.** Each Case opens as a season lead ("Three more letters overnight — Ozzie's stuck on the postmarks"); practice items render with clue-skinning IN CASE MODE ONLY (the frame calls them "the postmarks"; stems untouched); the cracked-stamp closes the lead with one story line. Item bank: zero rewrites. Authoring adds a per-case `storyWrapper` (open line, clue-skin labels, close line, season/thread tags — authored, scanned, ≤30s).
- **Walk-it is crew-taught.** Worked-example scripts re-voiced as the district specialist solving one on-screen ("Watch Marta price it — she draws the bars first"). Same faded-scaffold pedagogy; all authored (S3); Ozzie's occasionally include his classic near-miss, caught and corrected — Teach-Back foreshadowed inside teaching.
- **Signature story cases:** one per engine family per season is fully staged (bespoke art beat, dialogue choice, umbrella-clue eligibility). The 1-in-5 showpiece that makes the season feel fully diegetic without pricing every case like one.

## 5. RANKS, OUTFITS, EARNED PIECES
| Rank | Outfit transform | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee | Borrowed coat, deliberately too big | Marlowe's desk: "Let's see what you notice." |
| Junior Detective | First fitted coat, crew pin | First umbrella token |
| Detective | Signature coat in chosen colour, proper satchel | A specialist asks THEM for help |
| Senior Detective | Trench + hat option, brass magnifier | Ozzie's correction scene pays off |
| Chief Inspector | The long coat, HQ office key | Grey Umbrella reveal + the invitation |
Earned accessories (case milestones; never purchased, never randomised — D-laws): Ozzie's spare glasses, Marta's pencil, Gallery gloves, Archive bookmark, the grey umbrella itself (final). All parametric layers. Rank beats are prose Chapters per Laws 3–5 (second person, 2–4 min at reading age ≤9, full audio, one spot illustration at the head).

## 6. THE DETECTIVE'S BOARD EXAM (mock framing)
**Two locks, restating Laws 1–2 for this feature: the paper renders PLAIN exactly as specced — framing wraps the before and after, never the questions, timer or sheet. Rank and story gate on SITTING a Board exam, never its score; a child who scores 20% and one who scores 95% receive the identical story beat. Rank logic is provably score-blind (asserted in tests).**
- **Naming (skin only; code vocabulary unchanged):** Boss Case → "the Detective's Board Exam"; Half Boss Case → "the Board's Preliminary".
- **Invitation (fires on readiness unlock):** Marlowe, 2 panels: "The Board's asked for you. I told them you were ready — don't make a liar of me." Parent email subject: "[Name]'s been invited to sit the Detective's Board" + readiness picture + exam-day one-pager.
- **The hall:** one transition beat — Ashline's warmth dims into the Board's famously plain hall. "No tools in here, Detective. Just you — which is plenty." Then P4 rendering, untouched. **The plainness is diegetic: the story's explanation of the hall IS the pedagogy's explanation of Plain mode.** The real 11+ is, in-world, "your biggest Board exam yet" — the ritual (calm walk-in, plain room, pens down, identical warm beat after) is rehearsed exam-day psychology.
- **Completion beat, identical regardless of score:** "The Board has your paper. However it went, you sat it like a detective." Crew `proud`. Abandon path keeps its kind copy + "The Board reschedules all the time. So do we."
- **Results unchanged:** child sees strengths + one focus, never a number; parent sees score, trajectory, context copy as built.

## 7. PRODUCTION MODEL (£800–1,500 illustrator scope — the split that makes it fit)
**Illustrator delivers (the taste):** 1-page world style guide · character sheets for the six cast + avatar base (front/¾ + expression set per the aged-register laws: smaller eyes, asymmetric acting, competence props, no baby-coding) · avatar slot system (hair set incl. Black hairstyles done properly, 4+ skin tones, coat/accessory templates) · a chapter-head spot-illustration set (6–8 pieces) in the world style. Emerging children's/games illustrator; full IP assignment; our AI concept-exploration permitted pre-brief; delivered art human-made.
**We implement (the scale):** every rank outfit, accessory, panel layout and state acting as parametric vector layers from their sheets — consistency mathematical, marginal cost near zero.

## 8. KIDS' PANEL — ONE 20-MINUTE SESSION
0–5 min: three style boards — "which city do you want to work in?" · 5–12: cast sketches + one-line backstories — "who do you want on your crew?" + the embarrassment question verbatim to the 10–11s ("is this for kids younger than you?" — any yes from the older half kills that candidate) · 12–20: one playable story-case prototype (S1 codebreaker lead with clue-skinning + an Ozzie beat) — observe, don't rescue. Ezra: protocol debugger, excluded from counts.

## 9. BUILD DELTAS — PHASE 1 SCOPE (ratified; nothing in /packages/core learning logic changes)
**In phase 1 (prose infrastructure):** Case schema + `storyWrapper` (open line, clue-skin labels, close line, season/thread tags) · content type `Chapter` (prose body, audio ref, spot-image slot, seeded-word ids, rank/season/Board triggers, optional dialogue choice) · the Chapter Reader (calm reading surface honouring dyslexia/reduced-motion settings; tappable seeded words → definition card + Vault collect; audio playback; chapter shelf in the case files) · dialogue-choice component (2–3 options, cosmetic state only, no learning-state writes) · Board rename layer in child-facing strings · Board invitation email variant · Walk-it `voicedBy` field · score-blind rank test. **All behind a `story` feature flag; S1 content flips on when reviewed. NOT a 5A launch gate.**
**Deferred to art arrival / phase 2:** avatar cosmetic slots + rank-transform outfits, cast art integration, spot illustrations (placeholders until the illustrator delivers), signature story cases' bespoke staging.
Banned-vocab + claims scans cover all story content; Tension-Law and reading-age checks added to the content review checklist; chapter audio is a per-chapter release condition (Law 4).
