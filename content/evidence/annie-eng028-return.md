## 3. Standing note on the negation-built distractor

`en-plausible-not-stated` options built by negating the passage — "no line says his hip
hurts" — are inert by construction. Nothing in the window pulls toward them, so they are
eliminable without reading.

The tag is not the fault. The construction is. An option carrying this tag must be built
from something the lines make attractive, and then fail on being unstated. If the only
justification available is that the passage never mentions it, the option is not a
distractor and the item does not support four.

This is the mechanism behind the bank-wide inert-distractor rate, and the newest clusters
are the worst affected.
